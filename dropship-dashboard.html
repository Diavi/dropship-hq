🔍 Best Supplier Platforms — Find Winning Products
💡 How to Find a Winning Product
📈

Check demand first

Search the product on TikTok Thailand & Lazada. If top sellers have 100+ reviews and are "Sold Out" often — it sells.

💸

2.5x rule

Your selling price should be at least 2.5x your total cost (product + shipping). Below that, platform fees eat your profit.

⚡

Fast shipping wins

Thai buyers want 3–7 day delivery. Prioritize suppliers with Thailand warehouses or ePacket/AliExpress Standard Shipping.

🎯

Problem-solving products

Products that solve a visible problem (messy cables, charging, organization) get more TikTok views and convert better.

🔥

Check TikTok trends

Search #TikTokMadeMeBuyIt or browse TikTok Shop trending in Thailand. Find what's hot before listing.

🏆

Low competition niches

Search Lazada — if the first page has less than 50 sellers with the same product, you can compete. More = harder.

🔎 Product Research Step by Step
1

Browse TikTok Shop Thailand for trending products

Open TikTok app → search your niche (e.g., "desk organizer", "charging") → filter by "TikTok Shop" → look for videos with 100K+ views and a "Buy Now" button. These are proven sellers.

2

Check Lazada Bestsellers

Go to Lazada Thailand → browse a category → sort by "Top Sales". Products with 500+ reviews selling at a decent price are validated. Check how many sellers sell the same thing.

3

Search the product on CJDropshipping or AliExpress

Copy the product name → search on CJDropshipping first (better for dropshipping). Check price, shipping time to Thailand, and minimum order. Note your cost.

4

Calculate your margin

Go to the Profit Calculator tab. If margin is 30%+ after all fees → list it. Below 20% → find a cheaper supplier or don't sell it.

5

Order a sample first (optional but recommended)

For products over 500 THB, order 1 sample. Check quality, packaging, and actual shipping time before listing. Bad quality = bad reviews = account penalty.

🟠 Lazada Thailand — Rules

📝 Listing Rules

📏
Title Length

Max 255 characters. Must include brand name, product type, key feature. No ALL CAPS. No promotional words like "FREE" or "SALE" in title.

📸
Images

Main image: white background, no text/watermarks, square 1:1 ratio, min 500×500px. Max 8 images total. Lifestyle photos allowed for extra images.

📄
Description

Must be accurate. Cannot claim fake "original price". Must list all specs. Thai or English both accepted. HTML formatting is supported.

💰
Pricing

Price must be realistic. Lazada takes 1–5% commission + payment processing ~2%. Vouchers are optional but recommended to boost ranking.

🚚
Shipping

Must ship within 3 business days of order. Set accurate weight and dimensions for correct shipping fees. Use Lazada-partnered couriers for best rates.

🚫 Prohibited Items

BANNED Counterfeit / fake branded goods
BANNED Medical devices without FDA approval
BANNED Weapons, knives (any blade >10cm)
BANNED Alcohol & tobacco
BANNED Adult content
BANNED Unlicensed software
ALLOWED Electronics (need CE/FCC cert sometimes)
ALLOWED Power strips (must state safety standards)
🔴 TikTok Shop Thailand — Rules

📝 Listing Rules

📏
Title Length

Max 255 characters. Must be descriptive and relevant. No misleading info. Thai language preferred for Thailand market — or bilingual Thai/English.

📸
Images & Video

Min 1 image required, max 9. Video is highly recommended — products with video get 3–5x more views. Video max 60 seconds, shows product in use.

📄
Description

Must match what is shown in videos/images. Cannot make health/medical claims. No fake scarcity ("only 1 left" when it's not true). Specs must be accurate.

💰
Commission & Fees

TikTok takes 2–5% commission. Affiliate creator commissions are extra (0–30% — you set this). Free shipping threshold boosts conversion significantly.

🎯
Content Policy

Your videos cannot show competitor products. Cannot make unverified claims. Products shown in video must match exactly what is sold. No bait-and-switch.

🚫 Prohibited Items

BANNED Counterfeit goods (account banned immediately)
BANNED Health supplements with medical claims
BANNED Political / sensitive content products
BANNED Gambling-related items
BANNED Firearms, weapons, explosives
BANNED Items that violate Thai law
ALLOWED Electronics (power strips, chargers ✓)
ALLOWED Home & lifestyle products ✓

✅ Order Fulfillment Checklist
📦 When You Get an Order
  • Check order details (name, address, phone number)
  • Verify correct product variant (color, size, etc.)
  • Note the required shipping deadline
  • Check if buyer left special instructions
  • Place order with your supplier (AliExpress/CJ) with BUYER'S address
  • Screenshot/save the supplier order confirmation
  • Note estimated arrival date from supplier
🚚 Shipping & Tracking
  • Get tracking number from supplier
  • Enter tracking number into Lazada/TikTok platform
  • Mark order as "Shipped" on the platform
  • Send tracking info to buyer (if they ask)
  • Monitor tracking — check if package is moving
  • Follow up with supplier if no movement after 7 days
✍️ Before You List a New Product
  • Verified product demand (TikTok + Lazada search)
  • Calculated profit margin (minimum 25%)
  • Product is not in prohibited category
  • Have minimum 4–6 quality product images
  • Main image is white background, square
  • Title is under 255 characters with key features
  • Description includes all specs and features
  • Price set correctly (covers all fees + profit)
  • Shipping settings are accurate
  • Verified supplier can ship to Thailand
⭐ After Delivery — Get Reviews
  • Check tracking shows delivered
  • Wait 2–3 days then message buyer to confirm receipt
  • Politely ask buyer to leave a review
  • Respond to any review (good or bad)
  • If bad review: message buyer, offer solution
  • Record order in your sales tracker
